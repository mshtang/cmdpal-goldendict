from `MDict.Csharp/Utils/Lzo1x.cs`. That source carries a separate **GPL-2.0-or-later**
notice despite the repository's general MIT license. The adaptation is used under
GPL-3.0-or-later. Original minilzo: Markus F. X. J. Oberhumer; JavaScript port:
Alistair Braidwood. The adaptation removes compression, uses bounded buffers,
and implements overlapping backreferences byte by byte. Full GPLv3 text is in LICENSE.
